# 08 Grievance redressal SOP

As of 21 Nov 2026. Sources: DPDP Act s.8(10), s.13; Rule 14(3) (publish a response period, max 90 days; VERIFY); Intermediary Rules 2021 r.3(2) (officer details published; acknowledge 24 hours; dispose 15 days; VERIFY current text); SPDI Rules 2011 r.5(9) (grievance officer, one month).

## 2. Request types and handling

| Type | Examples | Verify identity how | Fulfil within |

| Access/summary | "what data do you hold" | Signed-in session, or email from the account address; for a child, parent proves control of consent email | 15 days |

| Breach/safety | | n/a | per 07 / 06 |

Never ask for more identity data than needed; never accept government ID by email.

## 3. Timeline
1. Log ticket (id, date, type, channel). **Acknowledge within 24 hours.**
2. Triage within 2 working days; assign.
3. **Resolve within 15 days** (policy; the law allows up to 90 days under DPDP Rules; do not use the full window by default).
4. Reply in plain language, in the requester's language where possible; for children, in simple terms and copy the parent where appropriate.
5. If cannot resolve: tell the requester why, and that they may approach the Data Protection Board of India (after exhausting this process) and, for intermediary matters, the Grievance Appellate Committee under the Intermediary Rules (VERIFY).
6. Close and record; retain ticket 3 years with minimal personal data.

## 4. Reporting
Monthly: tickets by type, median time to acknowledge/resolve, SLA breaches, repeat issues. Share with owner; keep for the Board if requested.

## 5. Template acknowledgement
"Hello, thanks for writing to NumberCraft. Your reference is [ID]. We received your [type] on [date]. We will reply within 15 days. If this is urgent or about a child's safety, reply with URGENT. - [GRIEVANCE OFFICER NAME]"
